The Rabbisir logo and its deterministic icon derivatives were supplied by the project owner and are
authorized for use in Rabbisir. They remain product identity assets and are not offered as reusable
marks under the source-code license. Their public file digests are pinned in
[`ASSET_MANIFEST.sha256`](ASSET_MANIFEST.sha256).

The final fixed-dark public site uses the 228×320 RGBA derivative at
`site/assets/rabbisir/rabbisir-mark-dark.png`. Its unchanged bytes have SHA-256
`3eb50dd54404dbedb76a1c1b6dae85fb058e274cff0629c500d6379fddb8ab7c`. The historical source
record states that this dark mark is an equal-height resize of the user-owned Rabbisir source with
RGB values inverted for dark-mode visibility. It is authorized only as Rabbisir product identity,
is not DeepSeek artwork, and is not offered for reuse under the source-code license. Its public-site
digest is pinned separately in [`SITE_ASSET_MANIFEST.sha256`](SITE_ASSET_MANIFEST.sha256) so it cannot
be confused with an App bundle resource.
